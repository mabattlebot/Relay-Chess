How Relay Chess works

1. Capture to relay. Capturing a piece gives you another move.
2. Use a different piece. A piece that already moved during your relay turn cannot move again during that chain.
3. Keep capturing. You can continue capturing with different pieces.
4. Quiet moves. A non-capturing move ends your turn.
5. Check ends the chain. If your move gives check, your turn ends immediately.
6. End whenever. You can voluntarily end your relay turn after a capture.

Normal chess rules still apply

Check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant, promotion, repetition, and the fifty-move rule are included.
