How Relay Chess works

Capture to relay. Every capture earns a bonus move with a different piece. Capture again and the chain continues.

Chains end on a quiet move, on giving check, or when you press End turn.

Amber squares mark pieces that already moved this turn. Everything else is standard chess.

Pieces: cburnett set by Colin M.L. Burnett (GPLv2+), from Lichess.
